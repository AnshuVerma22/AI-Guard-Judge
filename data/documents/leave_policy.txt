NimbusCloud Technologies — Employee Leave Policy

1. Annual Leave
All full-time employees are entitled to 18 days of paid annual leave per calendar year,
accrued at 1.5 days per completed month of service. Unused annual leave can be carried
forward to the next year, up to a maximum of 10 days. Any leave beyond this cap is
forfeited on December 31st unless approved for encashment by HR.

2. Sick Leave
Employees receive 10 days of paid sick leave per year. A medical certificate is required
for sick leave exceeding 2 consecutive days. Sick leave does not carry forward to the
next year and cannot be encashed.

3. Maternity Leave
Female employees are entitled to 26 weeks of paid maternity leave for the first two
children, and 12 weeks for the third child onward, in line with the Maternity Benefit
Act. Maternity leave can begin up to 8 weeks before the expected delivery date.

4. Paternity Leave
Male employees are entitled to 15 days of paid paternity leave, to be availed within
6 months of the child's birth.

5. Work From Home Policy
Employees may work from home up to 2 days per week with prior manager approval.
Full remote arrangements require VP-level sign-off and are reviewed quarterly.

6. Leave Application Process
All leave requests must be submitted via the HR portal at least 3 working days in
advance, except for sick leave, which may be applied for on the same day. Leave
approval is granted by the direct reporting manager within 24 hours of submission.

7. Public Holidays
NimbusCloud observes 12 public holidays per year as per the company holiday calendar,
published every January by HR.
