NimbusCloud Technologies — IT Security Policy

1. Password Requirements
All employee passwords must be at least 12 characters long, containing a mix of
uppercase, lowercase, numbers, and special characters. Passwords must be changed
every 90 days and cannot repeat the last 5 passwords used.

2. Multi-Factor Authentication
MFA is mandatory for all employees accessing company systems remotely, including
email, VPN, and internal dashboards. MFA is enforced via the Okta authenticator app.

3. Device Policy
Only company-issued laptops may be used to access production systems. Personal
devices may access email only through the approved Mobile Device Management (MDM)
application. Jailbroken or rooted devices are strictly prohibited from enrollment.

4. Data Classification
Company data is classified into three tiers: Public, Internal, and Confidential.
Confidential data (customer PII, financial records, source code) must never be
shared over unencrypted channels or personal email accounts.

5. Incident Reporting
Any suspected security incident, including phishing emails, lost devices, or
unauthorized access, must be reported to security@nimbuscloud.example within
1 hour of discovery. The security team aims to acknowledge reports within 30 minutes
during business hours.

6. Third-Party Access
External vendors requiring system access must undergo a security review and sign
a Data Processing Agreement (DPA) before credentials are issued. Vendor access is
automatically revoked after 90 days unless renewed.

7. AI Tool Usage
Employees may use approved AI coding assistants for internal development work.
Confidential source code and customer data must not be pasted into public,
non-enterprise AI tools.
