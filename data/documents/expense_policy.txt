NimbusCloud Technologies — Travel & Expense Reimbursement Policy

1. Travel Booking
All domestic and international travel must be booked through the company's approved
travel partner, TripDesk Corporate. Bookings made outside TripDesk will only be
reimbursed in exceptional cases with VP approval, and reimbursement will be capped
at the equivalent TripDesk fare.

2. Daily Allowance
Employees traveling for work receive a daily allowance (per diem) of ₹2,500 for
domestic travel and $75 for international travel, covering meals and incidentals.
Accommodation is billed separately and must not exceed ₹8,000/night domestically.

3. Client Entertainment
Client entertainment expenses up to ₹5,000 per event may be claimed without prior
approval. Expenses above this amount require written approval from the employee's
department head before the event.

4. Submission Deadline
All expense claims must be submitted through the Expensify portal within 15 days
of the expense being incurred. Claims submitted after 30 days will not be reimbursed
except in documented medical emergencies.

5. Reimbursement Timeline
Approved expense claims are reimbursed in the next payroll cycle, typically within
10 business days of approval.

6. Non-Reimbursable Items
Alcohol (outside approved client entertainment), personal entertainment, traffic
fines, and upgrades to business/first class without VP approval are not reimbursable.
